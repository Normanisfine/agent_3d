# Run summary: 42899712_a2-claude_s0 (precise static whitebox room)

## Final scene

- **Final `.blend`:** `runs/A2-claude/42899712/0/scene.blend`. It is a byte-identical copy of
  `third_party/aha-3d/scenes/42899712_a2-claude_s0/blender/room_v16.blend`
  (sha256 `3011ffa46560dce7642df13884db8c26342291be1aa4f9b7e392eb83f81caaf3`).
- The scene is generated by `scenes/42899712_a2-claude_s0/build/build_room.py`. A frozen copy is
  `build_room_v16_frozen.py`. It contains 78 semantic roots, each an Empty with a unique `instance_id`.
  Furniture and objects use `furniture/...` classes; architecture uses `structure/...`.
- Everything was built in code from beveled boxes, cylinders, rods, Bezier-rod stems and
  hemisphere shades. No image-to-3D generator was used, and no library assets were needed.
- Whitebox greyscale clay materials are used, with darker values for dark or black objects to keep them readable.
- The scene is authored directly in the Pi3X aligned room frame (identity model-to-world).
- Final stills (plan/front/side and six source-camera renders next to their source frames) are in
  `runs/A2-claude/42899712/0/final_views_v16/`. `stills_sheet.png` shows all of them on one sheet.
- `scene_v14_superseded.blend` and `final_views_v14_superseded/` are the earlier delivery copy,
  kept rather than overwritten.

## Stages that ran

1. **Intake:** `bash tools/indoor intake --request …/request.json --out …/intake` ran and is ready for authoring
   (whitebox, stills, precise, inspection camera, no people or motion).
2. **Claim and acceptance scope:** the existing claim `room42899712-a2-claude` (owner
   `claude-a2-42899712-s0`) was used. The scope was initialized with
   `acceptance init tasks/room42899712-a2-claude/scope.json` (reference reconstruction, render=stills).
3. **Reference reuse:** Pi3X inference was not rerun. I reused `perception/42899712/bundle`
   (aligned cameras, the TSDF `mesh/layers.npz`, masks) and the HD frames, cameras and depth.
   Room planes, fixture heights and object positions were measured from plan/elevation slices of the
   reference mesh. Exact positions came from Pi3X depth back-projection of source pixels: pillows, lamp
   shades, smoke detector, downlight and wall cabinet.
4. **Authoring:** I built 16 numbered revisions (`room_v1` … `room_v16`), each saved to a new file.
   After every edit I rendered matched source-camera comparisons (model / source / 50% overlay) from the
   cached cameras and inspected them myself. Each fix is recorded in the build script history
   (`build_room_vN.py`).
5. **Placement check:** `indoor check-placement` ran on every candidate. The final v16 has 0 errors.
   It has 31 warnings, mostly intended ceiling, wall and hanging items with no support below, plus
   rigid-body "unstable" diagnostics. Pillows only touch the sofa.
6. **Layout gate:** I used `layout_gate.prepare` through `build/run_layout_gate.py`, mirroring the
   pipeline's `ensure_layout`. It runs `render_blender` overlays, `export_model`, `object_outline`
   and `agent_review --all-objects`, with top/front/side views plus 16 native frames
   (0, 35, 69, 173, 208, 243, 277, 312, 346, 520, 693, 762, 901, 970, 1039, 1074) and
   `required_object_ids` for the 11 main items. A camera cache
   (`scenes/…/cameras/source_cameras_1024.npz`, built with `aha3d.workflow.camera.interpolate`)
   is bound into the spec.
7. **Candidate and technical check:** I registered candidate `2806d572…` for v16. The `scene` artifact
   is the v16 blend and the `render` artifact is the stills sheet. The final-view manifest is bound to
   the exact scene, cameras, configuration and video. `build/technical_check.py` passed and was
   recorded with `acceptance record-check … technical`. It confirmed:
   - the blend reopens;
   - all 78 roots are unique and every one has geometry;
   - all 47 inventory items are decided and every built ID exists;
   - there are 0 placement errors;
   - all views are present.

## Problems and how I handled them

- **Gate needed source focus for every object.** The south window is only visible in uncached frames
  (~990–1000), which have no reviewed cameras, so it is part of the `wall-west-living` root. Tiny
  ceiling fixtures needed enough projected pixels in the 364×266 cached views, so the downlights are
  2.8 cm surface trims.
- **The layout gate requires `camera_cache`.** I generated one from the reviewed cameras with the
  project interpolator.
- **Euler-mode bug on chair risers** caused floor penetration. I fixed it with explicit tilts and
  reran the placement check.
- **Sink inset caused penetrations.** I cut the countertop and carcass around the bowl.
- **Z-fighting strip** between the kitchen ceiling slab and the partition face. The kitchen ceiling
  now starts at the partition's kitchen face.
- **Reviewer outputs were sometimes schema-invalid.** I kept the raw outputs as
  `*_raw_invalid.json` and did not edit or fabricate any judgment.
- **The GPU was barely used.** Only Blender Workbench renders ran, one job at a time; `nvidia-smi`
  was checked before them.

## Skipped or out of scope

- **Hallway beyond the doorway:** other hallway doors, walls and the black item near the hallway
  door are outside the room. Only the hallway floor stub and the floor picture were built.
- **Small details not modeled:** wall sockets near the microwave cable, power cables, skirting boards,
  door hinges and the windows' outside brick.
- **Approximations:**
  - soft furnishings (pillows, sofa, oven glove, towel) are beveled boxes;
  - the dining table base is a column rather than a glass cross base;
  - the oven handle is straight;
  - unseen backs and interiors are simple completions;
  - dimensions are in predicted Pi3X metres and not calibrated.
