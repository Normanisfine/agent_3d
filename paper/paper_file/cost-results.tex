\subsection{Time and inference cost}
\label{sec:cost}
The extra coverage from Inventory came with a longer observed runtime.
Across the same three CA-1M rooms, All objects averaged 51.4 minutes and
Inventory averaged 81.9 minutes (Table~\ref{tab:existing-cost}). Jobs shared
compute, so these timings also reflect contention. Selective generation had
the lowest estimated driver cost and recovered 18 small objects by box overlap,
compared with 21 for Inventory.

Only three of the 15 saved CA-1M reconstructions passed AHa-3D's own review.
Two were baseline reconstructions, which correctly placed one of the 296
targets under 50\,cm in their rooms, so passing review said little about
small-object coverage. The token estimates cover the driving agent, including
resumed work. A run averaged 176 agent turns. Because the agent re-reads its
conversation on every turn, it consumed 31.7 million input tokens, 99\% read
from cache, against 0.14 million output tokens. Computing
cost per accepted room would also require complete costs for preprocessing,
hardware and reviewers, including those incurred on failed runs.
