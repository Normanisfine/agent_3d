\subsection{Time and token cost on CA-1M}
\label{sec:cost}
Across six held-out rooms, All objects correctly placed 71 small targets
compared with six for the baseline, at a lower mean token cost per room
(\$12.36 versus \$14.13) and a similar runtime (59 versus 62 minutes).
Checklist placed 142 targets on its first run and 155 on its second. Its
mean cost per room varied from \$13.28 to \$20.15 across the two runs,
and mean runtime rose from 79 to 113 minutes (Table~\ref{tab:six-rooms}).
These estimates cover the reconstruction agent. A run averaged 196 turns
and processed 39.1 million input tokens, 99\% of them cached.

Passing the agent's scene review was a poor indicator of small-object
placement. The review accepted five of six baseline scenes, which together
correctly placed just 5 of 527 targets under 50\,cm. It accepted only one
of the 24 scenes produced by All objects, Inventory and the two Checklist
runs, although each variant improved small-object placement in every room.
