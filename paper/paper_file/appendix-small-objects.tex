\section{Small objects: additional results}
\label{app:small}
\textbf{Box overlap and fixed scale.} Besides the center-and-size test of
Eq.~\ref{eq:center-size}, we score each match by box overlap,
$\operatorname{IoU}_{3\mathrm{D}}(P,G)\ge 0.25$, which exposes a 10\,cm shift
that puts a thin object beside its target, and we repeat the camera fit with
$s=1$ to test the reconstruction's own scale
(Table~\ref{tab:small-placement}). Relative to All objects, Inventory's net
gain was five placements by center and size, one by fitted-scale overlap and
zero by fixed-scale overlap.

\textbf{Inventories on development rooms.}
Table~\ref{tab:inventory} scores inventories on their own in five development
rooms, treating each inventory box as a placed object. Boxer's boxes passed
center and size for 10\% of the 216 targets under 20\,cm, and SAM~3 with a
fixed category list for 13\%. Room-specific categories raised this to 61\%.
The revised inventory lists 2{,}295 entries against 745, and the share that
match a target fell from 34\% to 19\%: it contains duplicates and object
parts. One refrigerator door covered with magnets and photographs holds 112
of the 216 smallest targets; without that room, the gain is from 18\% to
40\%. With every box shifted horizontally by a random 0.5 to 1.5\,m, about
1\% passed. On the six held-out rooms, scored after the Checklist settings
were fixed, the revised inventory placed 32\% of targets under 20\,cm and
41\% of those from 20 to under 50\,cm, against 6\% and 25\% for Boxer.

\textbf{Runs and renders.} One Checklist run in room 2 ended without
external review when the reviewer's quota ran out. Under a rule that does not
look at the score, we repeated it; the excluded run placed 36 targets under
50\,cm against 33 for the repeat. The comparison
renders in Figures~\ref{fig:qual} and~\ref{fig:qual-all} were made after the
runs from the saved scenes and were not seen by the agent or its reviewer.
The appearance pass is a separate agent run on the saved Checklist scene
that may assign materials and lights but may not move, add or remove
geometry.
